## 🚀 How to Get Started (2 minutes)

### Step 1: Open the App
Go to this URL in your browser:
```
https://your-deployed-url.com
```

### Step 2: Sign In
1. Click the **"Login"** tab
2. Enter your **Participant ID** (e.g., **P01**, **P02**, etc.)
3. Click **"Sign In"**

**Your login details:**
- Participant ID: **[YOUR_ID]** (sent separately)
- No password needed!

### Step 3: Make Predictions
1. You'll see all 104 World Cup matches
2. For each match, predict:
   - **Winner or Draw** (Team A, Draw, Team B)
   - **Score** (goals for each team)
3. For knockout matches (after group stage), also predict which team advances
4. Click **"Save All Predictions"** when done

### Step 4: Tournament Picks (Optional but Rewarded!)
1. Go to **"Tournament Picks"** tab
2. Predict:
   - World Cup winner
   - Runner-up
   - Golden Ball winner (best player)
   - Golden Boot winner (top scorer)
3. Click **"Save Tournament Picks"**

### Step 5: Check Leaderboard
1. Go to **"Leaderboard"** tab
2. See your rank and points
3. See all other participants' standings

**That's it! You're ready to compete!** 🏆

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: I forgot my Participant ID?
**A:** Check the email you received from admin, or contact them directly.

### Q: Can I change my prediction after submitting?
**A:** Yes, as long as the match hasn't kicked off yet! Just update and save again.

### Q: Can I predict if I paid late?
**A:** Yes! You can predict any match before its kickoff time.

### Q: What if there's a tie?
**A:** Tie-breaking rules (in order):
1. Highest total points
2. More exact score predictions
3. Higher knockout stage points
4. More correct tournament bonuses
5. Prize split equally

### Q: How do I know match times in my timezone?
**A:** All times shown in **Nepal Time (NPT)**.
- Convert to your timezone if needed
- Set phone reminders!

### Q: Can I see other people's predictions?
**A:** No! Your predictions are private. Only admin sees details.

### Q: What if the match goes to penalties?
**A:** Penalties don't change the score-based points.
- Group stage: 3 points for correct result only
- Knockout: 5 points for correct advancing team
- Exact score must match after regular/extra time

### Q: How are prizes paid?
**A:** Admin will contact winners after tournament ends.
- Bank transfer (preferred)
- Check
- Cash (if in-person)

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

1. Check your Participant ID (case-sensitive)

### "My predictions aren't saving"

- Use exact spelling as shown on app
- If not listed, it might be a TBD team (finalized later)
